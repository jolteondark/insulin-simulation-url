# Strict Basal+Regular dose pattern — session-level interpretation

The pooled 17-day means are dominated by session 2074 (12/17 days), so pooled B/L/D means must not be interpreted as population-level meal requirements without checking session-level direction.

Using the extracted actual prandial regular doses:

| session | days | mean B (U) | mean L (U) | mean D (U) | B/L |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2021_0_20211013 | 3 | 5.33 | 3.67 | 4.00 | 1.45 |
| 2025_0_20210506 | 1 | 14.00 | 10.00 | 12.00 | 1.40 |
| 2035_0_20210629 | 1 | 13.00 | 6.00 | 6.00 | 2.17 |
| 2074_0_20210707 | 12 | 12.17 | 8.58 | 8.36* | 1.42 |

*Dinner available on 11/12 days.

Direction is consistent across all 4 sessions: breakfast regular dose > lunch regular dose. This is not solely an artifact of the 2074 session dominating the pooled day count.

Caution: n=4 sessions is very small; this supports a time-of-day treatment requirement hypothesis but does not justify fixing a population breakfast-resistance parameter yet. Candidate explanations include morning insulin resistance/dawn-related physiology, meal carbohydrate composition not captured by food-weight proxy, correction dosing from elevated pre-breakfast glucose, and regimen-specific clinical practice.

Decision: do not alter physiology yet. First quantify whether B/L dose ratio persists after accounting for pre-breakfast glucose and meal amount, and obtain basal/TDD context.
