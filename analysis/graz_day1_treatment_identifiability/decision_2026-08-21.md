# Graz Day-1 identifiability decision — 2026-08-21

## Decision

Do **not** use Schaupp et al. 2015 / Graz CGM Day-1 aggregate outcomes as a hard physiology acceptance/rejection gate for the frozen mass-action100 + CR V2 width10/reserve1 candidate.

The dataset remains useful as a **treatment-context stress test** and descriptive external benchmark.

## Why

The frozen physiology was held fixed while only plausible, outcome-blind individual insulin-allocation assumptions were varied. N=8000 generated patients were baseline entropy-weighted to the reported Graz age/BMI/duration/weight summary. Day-1 outputs changed materially:

Published Graz Day-1 CGM target: mean 156.6 mg/dL, CV 39.6%, <70.2 2.6%, TIR 67.7%, >180 29.7%, >250.2 8.6%.

This range is too large for aggregate cohort insulin means to identify physiology. Choosing the arm closest to Graz would itself be outcome tuning and is prohibited.

## Additional context limitation

The Schaupp CGM analysis pooled patients from multiple registered studies (NCT01407289, NCT01766752, NCT01932775) and therefore does not correspond to one uniquely reconstructable treatment controller. Related Graz GlucoTab cohorts included several independently managed wards and both acute and scheduled admissions. Published aggregate insulin summaries do not preserve patient-level covariance among weight, home insulin use, renal function, illness acuity, starting TDD, correction doses, nutrition, and day-to-day dose changes.

The current experimental inpatient trajectory weights are also Emory sensitivity anchors, not a validated Graz acuity distribution. They must not be tuned to Graz glucose outcomes.

## Metric-definition check

The Graz Table 2 percentages are based on CGM glucose-value distributions; Day-1 proportions are therefore comparable to pooled equal-duration simulated Day-1 proportions. The primary mismatch is not explained by a simple TIR/TBR denominator error. CV aggregation may differ slightly (pooled SD/mean versus mean patient CV), but this cannot explain the large TIR/TBR differences.

## Frozen candidate remains frozen

Do not change:
- prandial mass-action reference = pre-existing 100 mg/dL threshold,
- CR V2 width = 10 mg/dL,
- CR reserve = 1,
- prandial/basal PK,
- basal potency 0.20,
- stress trajectories,
- phenotype weights,
- meal-mismatch probabilities,
- insulin scale,

in response to Graz.

## Next validation requirement

Prefer an independent T2DM inpatient dataset/cohort with either:
1. patient-level CGM plus patient-level insulin/nutrition exposure, or
2. a single fully specified treatment protocol plus enough baseline/context stratification to reconstruct treatment exposure without outcome-based tuning.

Until then, Graz is recorded as **context-limited / non-identifying external evidence**, not a physiology rejection.
