# Decision: strict Basal+Regular dose interpretation — 2026-08-20

## Findings

- 17 strict Basal+Regular days contained 50 recorded subcutaneous regular-insulin injection events.
- All 50 events occurred within -90 to +30 minutes of a recorded meal.
- Off-meal regular-insulin events: 0.
- Meals with multiple regular injections in the same meal window: 0.
- Therefore the high observed regular-insulin TDD is not explained by separately recorded between-meal correction injections.

## Important limitation

The Shanghai source does not label a meal-window injection as nutritional versus correction. A clinician may have incorporated a correction component into the single premeal dose. Therefore meal-window dose must not be interpreted as pure carbohydrate coverage.

## Dose–glucose coupling clue

Within the long 2074 session, same-day dose versus premeal glucose correlation was strongest at breakfast (approximately r=0.77), weaker at lunch/dinner (~0.37–0.41). Patient-centered pooled breakfast coupling across repeated-session data is approximately r=0.43. Previous-day glucose -> next-day dose association is much weaker for breakfast (~0.21 in 2074), so simple next-day titration alone does not explain the breakfast pattern.

## Model decision

1. Do not multiply physiological insulin potency or uPerG by ~1.8 to match the raw Shanghai regular TDD.
2. Do not treat strict-group regular TDD as pure nutritional insulin requirement.
3. Keep physiology/kernel unchanged for now; mean breakfast delta120 already matches the strict observed target.
4. Keep the treatment-policy layer separate and allow a premeal order to conceptually contain both nutritional and correction components.
5. Next priority is to estimate how much of the single meal-window dose can be explained by premeal glucose correction versus a patient-specific baseline prandial requirement, preferably with within-patient analysis and shrinkage because n=4 sessions is small.

Main remains frozen; this decision applies only to v2/state-space-minimal.
