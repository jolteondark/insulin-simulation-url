# Decision: strict Basal+Regular baseline prandial requirement

Using within-session, within-meal dose-vs-premeal-glucose slopes as an exploratory upper-bound correction component, doses were standardized to premeal glucose 120 mg/dL. Negative slopes were treated as zero correction.

Manual cross-check from the 17 strict days gives session-level baseline prandial requirement:
- 2021: 0.274 U/kg/day
- 2025: 0.522 U/kg/day
- 2035: 0.373 U/kg/day
- 2074: 0.352 U/kg/day
- session-equal mean: **0.380 ± 0.104 U/kg/day**

This is essentially unchanged from the observed strict prandial requirement (~0.376 U/kg/day). Therefore the gap versus the current matched model (~0.203 U/kg/day) is not explained by off-meal correction dosing or by a simple premeal-glucose correction component embedded in the meal dose.

Do not increase physiological insulin potency or alter the glucose kernel on this basis. The remaining discrepancy most likely reflects one or more of: unobserved carbohydrate amount, regular-insulin-specific dosing conventions/PK, treatment-selection effects not captured by static matching, or an inconsistency between the simulator's meal-dose mapping and the real-world dose scale. Next step: directly compare observed meal carbohydrate proxies and model meal carb assumptions, and test whether the implied observed g/U ratio is plausible before changing the dosing policy.

Main remains untouched.
