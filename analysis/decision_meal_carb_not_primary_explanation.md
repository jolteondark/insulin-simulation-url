# Decision: meal carbohydrate underestimation is unlikely to explain strict Basal+Regular dose gap

Date: 2026-08-20

The strict Basal+Regular subgroup requires substantially more prandial insulin than the current model, while basal dose/kg and breakfast delta120 mean are already close.

Direct inspection of recorded meals argues against the model's 50/70/60 g meal plan being grossly too small as the primary explanation. In session 2074, recorded meals commonly contained only modest staple portions (e.g. breakfast bread/rice-cake portions and lunch/dinner coarse grain around 100 g food weight) despite regular doses around 15/10/10 U. Session 2035 similarly shows staple portions around 136-137 g food weight on recorded days, not evidence of unusually high carbohydrate exposure.

Food records are incomplete and food weight is not carbohydrate grams, so these data cannot calibrate absolute ICR. However, the direction is sufficient to reject 'hidden very large carbohydrate meals' as the leading explanation for the ~0.20 vs ~0.38 U/kg/day prandial-dose gap.

Do not inflate meal carbohydrate solely to force dose agreement. Next compare formulation/treatment exposures within the same patient/session where possible (SC regular vs rapid analogue or CSII) to distinguish insulin formulation/effect mapping from patient physiology and prescribing policy.
