# UOM inter-meal context analysis (2026-08-19)

Source: ManchesterCSCoordinatedDiabetesStudy-V1.0.3 uploaded ZIP.

Goal: quantify real-world carbohydrate/snack and bolus events between pseudo-check windows to assess mismatch with fixed 3-meal / 3-bolus simulation.

All available nutrition and bolus subject-day records were aggregated across 07:00-12:00 and 12:00-18:00 windows (1378 subject-days in union of available logs; descriptive context analysis, not restricted to the prior CGM-completeness cohort).

## 07:00-12:00
- Any carbohydrate event: 59.2% of subject-days
- Median carb events: 1; mean 0.80
- Total carbohydrate: median 13 g; mean 25.3 g
- Any snack-type carbohydrate event: 17.4%
- Snack carbs: mean 3.29 g/day across all days in window
- >=2 carbohydrate events: 18.5%
- Any bolus: 57.5%
- Bolus events: median 1; mean 0.81
- Total bolus: median 2.87 U; mean 2.96 U
- >=2 bolus events: 17.7%
- Any sub-1-U bolus: 8.4%
- Among carb-positive days, 29.4% contained a snack-type event.

## 12:00-18:00
- Any carbohydrate event: 75.4% of subject-days
- Median carb events: 1; mean 1.09
- Total carbohydrate: median 46 g; mean 48.7 g
- Any snack-type carbohydrate event: 28.2%
- Snack carbs: mean 7.48 g/day across all days in window
- >=2 carbohydrate events: 26.9%
- Any bolus: 73.8%
- Bolus events: median 1; mean 1.37
- Total bolus: median 5.0 U; mean 6.11 U
- >=2 bolus events: 35.1%
- Any sub-1-U bolus: 12.3%
- Among carb-positive days, 37.4% contained a snack-type event.

## Interpretation
The UOM 12:00 and 18:00 glucose fingerprints are not generated by a clean single-meal/single-bolus interval. Snacks/additional carbohydrates and multiple boluses are common, especially 12:00-18:00. Therefore fitting the fixed three-meal simulator directly to the unconditional UOM four-check means risks forcing physiology kernels to compensate for missing behavioral context.

This supports stratified conditional validation next: compare UOM four-check transitions in clean windows (single primary meal, no snack, single >=1 U meal bolus, no extra bolus) versus context-rich windows. Core meal/rapid physiology should be calibrated preferentially to clean windows; snack/correction behavior should be modeled as an exogenous context layer rather than absorbed into physiologic kernels.
